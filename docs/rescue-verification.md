# How to Verify Legitimate 501(c)(3) Animal Rescues Before Submitting an Adoption Application

*Published November 2026 · Adopter Due Diligence*

With thousands of independent foster networks operating online across the United States, spending five minutes verifying an organization’s nonprofit credentials protects adopters from retail puppy brokers disguising commercial sales as rescue rehoming.

## Three Public Records Every Reputable Rescue Shares

Transparent animal welfare organizations publish their federal Employer Identification Number (EIN), state Department of Agriculture rescue registration where required (such as Colorado PACFA, Georgia, or Illinois), and direct veterinary clinic references. Legitimate rescues never demand wire transfers or peer-to-peer cash apps prior to an approved application and live video or in-person meet-and-greet.

Prospective guardians can cross-reference vetted rescue groups through [AdoptAnimaux partner associations](https://adoptanimaux.com/en/associations) and browse regional facilities inside the [United States adoption directory](https://adoptanimaux.com/en/adoption/directory/us).

## Red Flags vs. Healthy Screening Practices

| Evaluation Area | Healthy 501(c)(3) Standard | Broker / Scam Warning Sign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medical Paperwork | Clinic-headed rabies certificate & microchip transfer | Handwritten vaccine booklet with no DVM license |
| Return Safety Net | Contractual lifetime return-to-rescue clause | No return address or refusal to reclaim if needed |
| Fee Structure | Clear schedule tied to spay/neuter & wellness costs | Dynamic "shipping" or "crate insurance" surcharges |

## Checklist Prior to Signing an Adoption Contract

- Look up the organization’s nine-digit EIN in the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search tool.
- Confirm whether spay or neuter surgery has already been completed or is scheduled under a refundable pediatric deposit.
- Keep digital copies of the signed transfer agreement and microchip manufacturer code before leaving the adoption event.

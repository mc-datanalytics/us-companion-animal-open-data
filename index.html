U.S. Companion Animal Adoption Listing Inventory & VBO Crosswalk Notes

Companion documentation and adopter readiness guides referencing the canonical U.S. dog and cat adoption listing datasets published on Adopt'Animaux.

Methodological Scope: All datasets referenced here measure active, location-ready adoption listing inventory represented in published U.S. city hubs (not shelter intake, adoption outcomes, euthanasia, or unique animal counts). Source-label crosswalks against the Vertebrate Breed Ontology (VBO, release 2026-04-15) use deterministic species-scoped Unicode NFKC exact matching against canonical labels and EXACT synonyms (excluding national breed populations VBO:0300020) and do not represent genetic breed verification.
